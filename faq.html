COLLECTION & PRODUCT04 QUESTIONS
What models are included?+
The client requested four models: RAPAX Tactical, Sport, Compact and Polymer Compact.
Are the finishes, sights and grips configurable?+
The client stated that grip, sight and finish options will be offered, with some affecting price and others only appearance. Exact model-specific choices and prices have not yet been delivered.
Are accessories part of the site?+
Yes. The confirmed limited accessory categories are barrels and extra magazines.
Where can I find technical specifications?+
The model comparison and individual product pages summarize information from the client-supplied RAPAX technical sheet and catalog.
ORDERS & REQUIREMENTS04 QUESTIONS
Is online checkout part of the final website scope?+
Yes, the agreed WordPress / WooCommerce project includes full online checkout. This design prototype excludes the checkout page as requested.
What is the planned FFL information step?+
The client requested FFL dealer name, address and contact fields; the agreed scope also included license number and a 21+ age confirmation.
Are all U.S. states supported?+
The client listed California, New York, Illinois, Massachusetts, Maine and Connecticut as states to block in the planned checkout. Final operational policies require client review.
What payment gateway is planned?+
The client identified Authorize.net, subject to compatibility and final access for the WooCommerce integration.
BRAND & SUPPORT03 QUESTIONS
Who is the U.S. distributor?+
Hyperion Munitions is the exclusive U.S. importer and distributor for the RAPAX line.
Who makes RAPAX?+
RAPAX is manufactured by DECIMA / XDB in Italy.
Are the policies on this prototype final?+
No. The agreed scope calls for draft policy content to undergo final client legal review.
